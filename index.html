Alignment & Settings

  • Page Ranges: Specify exact pages to compare (e.g., "1-3, 5").
  • Center Align: Aligns both documents perfectly in the middle.
  • Top Left Align: Anchors both documents to the top left corner.
  • 3-Point Align: Manual calibration wizard. Click 3 matching points on both documents.
  • Markups: Choose whether to include embedded annotations.

How It Works

  1. Use Overlay Compare to compare revisions with vector accuracy, pan/zoom, and color-coded diffs.
  2. Use PDF Toolbox & Manager to view vertically stacked thumbnails with adjustable width, rotate, insert blank white sheets, flatten markups, extract, and delete pages locally.
  3. Use PDF Binder to upload multiple PDF files, re-order them using drag and drop, and merge into a single bound document.
